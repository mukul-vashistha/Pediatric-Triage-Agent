# LinkedIn Posts

Five posts connected to this project, matching the discussion-record.md entries L-D1 through L-D3 plus two additional posts not captured there.

## Post 1 (L-D1): Exploring AI for Pediatric Care, Making Decisions With Incomplete Information

Posted: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/activity-7494479362956685314-uBiZ

Introduces the original problem: an agent that must decide whether a child needs to be monitored at home, seen by a doctor, or taken to emergency care, when the parent's description of symptoms is incomplete and the true condition is never directly observable.

## Post 2 (L-D2): How AI systems adjust on the go based on the information they receive

Posted: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/activity-7500148338743410688-ZFpM

Introduces the idea of sequential evidence-gathering and belief updating, rather than making one fixed decision from the first piece of information available.

## Post 3 (L-D3): Why providing a lot of data might not always be the best choice

Posted: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/activity-7500560318205722624-L45R

Introduces the value-of-information idea: more evidence isn't automatically better once the cost of gathering it is actually counted.

## Post 4: AI Triage Agent System Flow

Posted: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/activity-7501321942151557121-PahV

Walks through the overall architecture: observation, hidden states, belief, evidence, posterior, information value, decision policy, action.

## Post 5: Addition of Safety Layer

Posted: https://lnkd.in/p/drdrgGuC

Describes adding a hardcoded safety override in front of the Bayesian pipeline for a small set of unambiguous danger signs, and why this was added after a failure analysis rather than from the start.

## Post 6: Full Paper Published

Posted: https://lnkd.in/p/dX-fxnKV

Announces the completed paper with the PDF attached. Summarizes the five hidden states, the value-of-information result (roughly 3x fewer wrong decisions at a higher information-gathering cost), and the real bug caught and fixed through failure analysis.
